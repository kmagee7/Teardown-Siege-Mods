description = A full team siege game mode for multiplayer. Attackers break through the defences and capture the objectives; defenders fortify and hold out until the timer runs out. It works on any map, and the Siege Keep map comes ready to play. HOW A MATCH PLAYS: Each round starts with a preparation phase where both teams can move and build, and attackers have no weapons and can't be hurt. An attacker standing in a zone with no defender inside fills its capture bar, and extra attackers speed it up. A defender inside pauses it, and an empty zone slowly drains it. An objective can also fall if enough of the floor under it is destroyed. Attackers win by reaching the points target. Defenders win if the timer runs out, or if reinforcements run out and every attacker is dead. Sides can swap each round, the map resets between rounds, and scores carry across the match. THE BOW: Everyone fights with a bow by default. Hold fire to draw and let go to shoot. A longer draw flies faster and flatter and hits harder, and a head hit does double damage. Plain arrows are unlimited. Press R to switch to fire arrows, which set wood alight; the host sets how many each player gets per round. There's a sword for close fighting too: left click swings it, and it chops into wood. Guns and modern tools are off by default. Prefer gunfire? The host can switch Weapons era to Guns. FORTIFICATIONS: Press B to build stone ramparts, low cover, brick walls, timber barricades and iron bulwarks, using a build-point budget. The host decides who can build and how strict placement is. LADDERS: Once the siege starts, attackers press L and carry a long ladder to the wall, walking slowly. It points where you look, and left click lets go. Lean it well and it holds; too upright, too shallow or off target and it falls, and that ladder is lost. The host sets how many each attacker gets per round. Defenders can push a ladder off from the top or shoot it down. MAP FEATURES: Maps can include working drawbridges and portcullises worked by levers, defender-only hoists, and fixed cannons and trebuchets. Siege Keep has all of these. Fixed guns need THE CANNON (Siege). BOTS: Short on players? Bot archers stand on the walls and in the trenches and shoot at enemies they can see, and bot crews work the fixed cannons and trebuchets. The host sets how many on each side and how skilled they are. Bots can be killed and come back after a while, and a player can take over any gun. They don't capture objectives or count toward winning a round. Bots need a map with posts marked, like Siege Keep. FOR THE HOST: A menu sets everything up in-game. Place objectives, spawns and wall sections anywhere, with layouts saved per map in named slots. Sliders and switches cover round time, preparation time, capture time, objective size and points, respawn time, fixed gun reload times, reinforcements, number of rounds, team balance, ammo and clearing small rubble. Allow or ban each weapon, including tools from other mods, and choose which cannon balls are allowed on cannons and on trebuchets separately, and how strong each one is. A wall tracker shows how much of each section is still standing, and a scoreboard tracks kills and deaths. Works best with THE CANNON (Siege). Everyone needs the same version of each mod.
