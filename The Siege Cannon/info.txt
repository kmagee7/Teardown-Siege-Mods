description = A version of seached's "THE CANNON" adapted for the Siege game mode, with fixed cannons and trebuchets added. Check out the original and Siege too! THE HAND CANNON: A cannon you carry. Press R to switch ball type, and left click to fire. BALL TYPES: Traditional is a heavy physics ball that bounces and rolls. Breaker tunnels through walls. Ricochet bounces off what it hits. Normal Explosion and Ultra Explosion blow up on impact. Ultra Ultra sets off a huge blast and a storm of small balls. Eraser lands, arms itself, then bursts into small balls. Full Auto fires rapidly while you hold the button. Tether Chain keeps the ball on a chain you can reel in and flick. Colossus is a giant ball. FIXED CANNONS: Maps can include mounted cannons on rail traverses. Look at one and press E to man it. A/D turns it, W/S raises and lowers the barrel, left click fires, R switches ball type and E leaves. They fire every ball type, reload more slowly than the hand cannon, have a metal shield in front of the gunner, and show an arc of where the shot will land. They can be destroyed. TREBUCHETS: Maps can also include trebuchets that throw the same balls. There is no range number or aiming arc. W/S makes the throw heavier or lighter, with the winch clicking as you go, so you learn the distance by trial and error. They take time to wind back between throws. BOT CREWS: In the Siege game mode, bots can man these guns on both sides, and a player can take over from one at any time. IN THE SIEGE GAME MODE: The host chooses which balls are allowed on cannons and on trebuchets separately, and sets the power of each. Each side can only man its own guns once the siege starts. Fixed guns only appear on maps built for them, such as Siege Keep.
