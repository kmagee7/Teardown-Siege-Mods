description = A huge concentric star fort built for the Siege game mode. Needs Siege, and THE CANNON (Siege) for the fixed guns. Objectives, spawns and wall sections are already set up, so you can start straight away. THE CASTLE: A low star-shaped outer wall with four arrow-head bastions stands on a raised plateau behind a sunken moat, with a higher inner wall behind it and a towering keep at the centre. Each wall fires over the one in front. Flared gun ports line the outer wall, and tall crowned towers rise from the bastions and the inner corners. GATES: A wooden drawbridge and an outer portcullis guard the road in, and a second portcullis guards the inner gate, set off to one side so nobody charges straight through. Defenders work them with levers. There are murder holes over both gate passages, and the bridge can be destroyed. THE OBJECTIVE: The Great Hall on the ground floor of the keep, with its door at the back behind a baffle wall. GETTING AROUND: Wall galleries, wall walks and tower stairs link the defences. Tunnels run from the hall to all four sides of the space between the walls, behind a grate defenders control. The keep has an inside stair, a broad stair up its back, bridges to the inner wall and a defenders-only hoist to the roof, and every tower has a hoist of its own. Bots can man the guns and hold posts on both sides. DEFENDERS: Nine spawn points, seven cannons on the outer wall and keep roof, and two trebuchets on the inner towers. ATTACKERS: Three camp spawn points, 32 cannons across stone gun batteries and redoubts, and six trebuchets behind them. Four of the cannons sit in stone pillboxes. Zigzag trenches, stone camp buildings and rolling ground give cover on the way in, and ladders can reach the walls if the bridge is up. Every attacker work can be destroyed. THE LAND: The castle stands on a headland with cliffs and sea on every side, so attackers can only come from the south. The field has a shallow layer of diggable soil, plus trees, bushes, rocks, grass and reeds at the water's edge. The map is split into smaller wall sections for performance, but it is still big, so expect it to take some horsepower.
